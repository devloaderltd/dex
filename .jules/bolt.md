## 2026-05-11 - Gas Optimization in StakingRewards updateReward modifier
**Learning:** In PurDexStakingRewards.sol, calling `earned(account)` inside the `updateReward` modifier redundantly recalculates `rewardPerToken()` even though `rewardPerTokenStored` is just updated to exactly that value on the previous line. This causes an unnecessary extra execution of `rewardPerToken()` logic (which involves `lastTimeRewardApplicable`, `block.timestamp`, and math).
**Action:** Inline the `earned` calculation inside `updateReward` to reuse the already-cached `rewardPerTokenStored` value instead of calling the external-facing `earned()` function. Additionally, `public` methods (`withdraw` and `getReward`) called internally (in `exit`) trigger double invocation of `nonReentrant` and `updateReward` modifiers. We can split these into `_withdraw` and `_getReward` internal functions to save gas on the `exit` path.
