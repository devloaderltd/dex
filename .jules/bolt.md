## 2024-05-15 - StakingRewards Internal Caching Optimization
**Learning:** Found redundant calculations in `PurDexStakingRewards.sol`. Inside `updateReward`, `earned(account)` recalculates `rewardPerToken()` even though `rewardPerTokenStored` is updated just one line before. Also, `exit()` makes two external-like calls to `withdraw` and `getReward`, running their modifiers redundantly and costing more gas.
**Action:** When implementing `updateReward` in StakingRewards, inline the `earned` calculation using the already-cached `rewardPerTokenStored`. For standard StakingRewards interfaces, convert `public` `withdraw`/`getReward` into `external` functions, and implement `internal` prefixed versions (`_withdraw`/`_getReward`) to be called natively within `exit()`, saving modifier re-evaluations and external routing costs.
